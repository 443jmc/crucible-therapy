How to Become a Crucible Therapist

To become a Crucible therapist, go to the International Crucible Education Center (ICTEC) and sign up for trainings.

ICTEC is the official training home. Their trainings and workshops page lists current offerings. They also run consultation groups if you want ongoing case work.

If you want to read first, a strong reading sequence usually starts with Passionate Marriage, then Intimacy & Desire, and then Brain Talk. More of Schnarch's books are listed on this site and at Crucible 4 Points. The training itself is at ICTEC.
