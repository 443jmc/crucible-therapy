Learning the Crucible Approach

If you are a clinician trying to understand Crucible Therapy, start by seeing how it differs from the models you already know. Then train with the people who carry the work forward. This site is an independent explainer of Dr. David Schnarch's Crucible Approach. Official training lives at ICTEC. Schnarch's source materials live at Crucible 4 Points.
